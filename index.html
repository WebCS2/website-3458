Quick Installation Guide

Get up and running with SourClient in under 2 minutes.

1

Install Fabric Loader

Download and run the Fabric Installer for Minecraft 1.21.11 or 26.1.2.

2

Drop Jar File

Place sourclient-1.0.0.jar into your .minecraft/mods folder.

3

Launch & Bind

Launch Minecraft with Fabric profile. Press Right Shift to open the ClickGUI screen.
